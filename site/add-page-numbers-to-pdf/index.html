Add page numbers to PDF

Number the pages of a PDF, or of several PDFs at once, at the top or bottom: on the left, in the center or on the right. Skip a cover or any other pages, then save the new PDF.

How to add page numbers to a PDF

  1. Open Add page numbers. Press the Open Add page numbers button at the top of this page. The tool opens in your browser, on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.
  2. Choose your PDFs. Press Choose PDFs and pick one PDF or several; on a computer you can also drop them on it. If a PDF asks for its password, type it and press Open PDF.
  3. Pick the place and size. Under Position, pick one of the six spots on the small page; it starts at Bottom center. Under Size, pick Small, Medium or Large.
  4. Skip pages if you need to. In Pages to skip, type pages such as 1 or 1-2,5, or leave it empty to number every page. Each page keeps its own number.
  5. Add the numbers. Change the name under File name if you like, then press Add page numbers. With several PDFs the button says how many, such as Add page numbers to 3 PDFs.
  6. Save the result. For one PDF, press Save to Downloads, or Save as… or Share where your browser offers them. For several, Save to Downloads saves them all and Share or save as ZIP puts them in one ZIP file.

What you can do with Add page numbers

  • Put the numbers at the top or bottom of the page, on the left, in the center or on the right: six places, picked on a small page.
  • Three sizes: Small (9 pt), Medium (12 pt) and Large (16 pt). The numbers are black and sit at most half an inch from the edges of the page, closer on small pages.
  • Pages to skip leaves pages without a number, such as a cover (1) or 1-2,5. Each page keeps its own number: skip page 1, and page 2 is numbered 2.
  • Numbers go where you see the top or bottom of the page, even on pages turned sideways.
  • The numbers are real text in the PDF, not pictures.
  • Number several PDFs the same way in one go. Each gets its own File name, and you can save them one by one or as one ZIP file.
  • PDFs locked with a password open once you type it, and the new PDF has no password.
  • The rest of the PDF stays as it was, with its form fields, comments, bookmarks and links.

Your files stay on your device

Usugami works on your files in your browser, on your device, and nothing is uploaded: the files you pick and the PDFs it makes never leave your device.

There are no ads, no tracking and no cookies, and you don’t need an account. Tools you have used once keep working offline.

Questions

Can I leave the cover page without a number?

Yes. Type 1 in Pages to skip. Each page keeps its own number, so page 2 is still numbered 2.

Can I number several PDFs at once?

Yes. Choose several PDFs, or add more with Add PDFs. Every PDF is numbered the same way, and pages to skip are skipped in each PDF that has them.

What do the numbers look like?

Each page gets just its own number, such as 7, in black, in the place and size you choose.

Where do the numbers go on pages turned sideways?

Where you see the top or bottom of the page. Usugami places them on each page as a PDF viewer shows it, not as it is stored.

Is Usugami free?

Yes. Usugami is free and open source, with no ads, no account and nothing to buy.

Does Usugami put its name on my PDFs?

Only if you turn on the Usugami watermark switch on the result screen, which adds a small “Usugami” in the corner of each page. It’s off unless you turn it on.

Which browsers does it work in?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on phones, tablets and computers. Some buttons, such as Share and Save as…, show only in browsers that support them. On an Android phone you can also install the Usugami app.
