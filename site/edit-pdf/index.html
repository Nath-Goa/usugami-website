Edit PDF files

Add text, drawings, highlights, shapes and pictures to the pages of a PDF, right in your browser. Move and resize what you add, then save it all as a new PDF.

  • Open Edit PDF

    Works on phones and computers. Your files stay on your device.

  • Get the Android app

    Free, for Android 8.0 or newer. About 3 MB.

How to edit a PDF

  1. Open the tool. Press the Open Edit PDF button at the top of this page. The tool opens in your browser, on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.
  2. Choose your PDF. Press Choose PDF and pick the PDF, or drop it there on a computer. If it’s protected with a password, Usugami asks for it first.
  3. Add something to the page. Press Text, Draw, Highlight, Shape or Picture. For text, type it in the box that opens and press Done.
  4. Move it and change it. Drag what you added into place, or tap it to choose it and use its controls, such as Size and the colors. While Draw or Highlight is on, dragging draws, so press it again to stop drawing first. In a PDF with more than one page, the page previews take you to another page.
  5. Save the new PDF. Check the File name and press Save PDF, then Save to Downloads. Where your browser can share files, Share sends it to another app.

What you can do with Edit PDF

  • Add text on one or more lines, from 6 up to 96 pt, in black, blue, red, green, yellow or white. Edit text changes the words of text you added.
  • Draw freehand with a finger, a pen or the mouse. Highlight is see-through and blends with the page, so black text under it stays black.
  • Shape adds a rectangle, a filled rectangle, an ellipse, a line or an arrow. Filled fills a rectangle or an ellipse.
  • Picture adds an image from your device, and on a computer you can also drop or paste one onto the page. It goes in upright, without hidden details such as the photo’s location.
  • Drag what you added to move it and use its handles to resize it, or use the move buttons and the Size slider. On a computer, the arrow keys move it (with Shift, 1 pt at a time).
  • Undo takes back up to 50 changes, turning to the page each one was on, and Redo brings them back.
  • Text you add stays real text that you can search and select. Drawings and shapes are saved as lines and curves, not pixels, so they stay sharp when you zoom in.
  • Pages you add nothing to stay exactly as they were. On the pages you add to, form fields and comments under what you add become part of the page, so your additions sit on top of them as in the preview. A filled shape only covers what’s under it, which stays in the file. To remove something for good, use Redact PDF.

Your files stay on your device

Usugami works on your files right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: the files you pick and the PDFs it makes never leave your device.

There are no ads, no tracking and no cookies, and you don’t need an account. Tools you have used once keep working offline while your browser keeps their files; a text-recognition language or font used for the first time needs the internet.

Questions

Can I change the text that’s already in my PDF?

No. Edit PDF adds new text, drawings, shapes and pictures on top of a page, and what is already on the page stays in the file. A filled shape can cover something, but to remove it for good, use Redact PDF.

Is the text I add searchable?

Yes. It’s saved as real text in the Noto Sans fonts that come with Usugami, so you can search and select it, and Hindi works too. Characters those fonts don’t have, such as Chinese or emoji, are drawn by your browser as a picture, with the text kept invisibly over it so it can still be found. If your device has no font for some characters, Usugami asks you to try different text.

How do I add an image to a PDF?

Press Picture and choose an image, or on a computer drop or paste one onto the page. It appears on the page ready to drag into place, and its handles or the Size slider change its size.

Can I add things to more than one page?

Yes. Pick another page from the page previews and add to it. Pages with additions are marked, and Save PDF puts everything into one new PDF.

Is Usugami free?

Yes. Usugami is free and open source, with no ads, no account and nothing to buy.

Does Usugami put its name on my PDFs?

Not on its pages, unless you turn on the Usugami watermark switch on the result screen, which adds a small “Usugami” in the corner of each page. Once on, it stays on for the PDFs you make next until you turn it off. The PDF’s document properties do name Usugami as the program that made it.

Which browsers does it work in?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on phones, tablets and computers. Some buttons, such as Share and Save as…, show only in browsers that support them. On an Android phone you can also install the Usugami app.
