Delete PDF pages

Delete pages takes the pages you don’t need out of a PDF. Tap them in the page grid or type their numbers, see how many will go, then save the new PDF.

How to delete pages from a PDF

  1. Open the tool. Press the Delete pages from a PDF button at the top of this page. The tool opens in your browser, on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.
  2. Choose your PDF. Press Choose PDF and pick the PDF (on a computer you can also drop it on that card). If it has a password, Usugami asks for it.
  3. Mark the pages to delete. Tap each page to delete in the grid, or type them in Pages to delete, such as 2,5-7. The pages you tap fill in the field, and the line below it counts them, as in “To delete: 4 of 12”.
  4. Save the new PDF. Check the File name and press Delete pages. Then press Save to Downloads or Open; Share and Save as… also show where your browser offers them.

What you can do with Delete pages

  • Type page numbers counted from 1, separated by commas, with a hyphen for a range: 2,5-7 deletes pages 2, 5, 6 and 7. Spaces and order don’t matter.
  • Or tap pages in the grid. Each page you mark fades behind a delete sign, and the field fills in for you, with runs written as ranges.
  • A PDF keeps at least one page: with every page marked, Usugami says “Keep at least one page.”
  • If Usugami can’t use the numbers you typed, it says why when you press Delete pages, for example “Write ranges from low to high, such as 3-8.”
  • The pages you keep still have their bookmarks, links, form fields and page labels (such as “iv”).
  • The new PDF is named after yours, such as Report_pages_deleted.pdf, and you can change the File name first.

Your files stay on your device

Usugami works on your files right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: the files you pick and the PDFs it makes never leave your device.

There are no ads, no tracking and no cookies, and you don’t need an account. Tools you have used once keep working offline while your browser keeps their files. A few parts download the first time they’re needed, such as a text-recognition language, a font or the HEIC photo decoder, and need the internet then.

Questions

How do I delete several pages at once?

Mark them all before you save: tap each one in the grid, or type a list such as 2,5-7 in Pages to delete. Usugami removes them all in one go and makes one new PDF.

Can I delete every page of a PDF?

No. A PDF needs at least one page, so leave at least one page unmarked.

Are deleted pages really removed from the file?

Yes. Their text, the pictures only they used, their form fields and comments aren’t left hidden in the new PDF.

Can I delete pages from a password-protected PDF?

Yes. Usugami asks for the password when you choose the PDF. The new PDF has no password, and the result screen tells you so.

Is Usugami free?

Yes. Usugami is free and open source, with no ads, no account and nothing to buy.

Does Usugami put its name on my PDFs?

Not on its pages, unless you turn on the Usugami watermark switch on the result screen, which adds a small “Usugami” in the corner of each page. Once on, it stays on for the PDFs you make next until you turn it off. The PDFs it makes do name Usugami in their document properties, as the program that made them.

Which browsers does it work in?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on phones, tablets and computers. Some buttons, such as Share and Save as…, show only in browsers that support them. On an Android phone you can also install the Usugami app.
