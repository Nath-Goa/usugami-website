How to convert EPUB to PDF

  1. Open the tool. Press the Open EPUB to PDF button at the top of this page. The tool opens in your browser, on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.
  2. Choose your e-books. Press Choose EPUB files and pick one or more .epub books. On a computer, you can also drop the files there.
  3. Pick a page size. Under Page size, keep A5, about the size of a paperback, or choose A4, which suits printing.
  4. Convert to PDF. Check the File name of each book, then press Convert to PDF. With several books the button says how many, and each one becomes a PDF of its own.
  5. Save your PDF. On the result screen, press Open or Save to Downloads, or Share and Save as… where your browser offers them. Several PDFs can also go into one ZIP file with Share or save as ZIP.

What you can do with EPUB to PDF

  • Each page size has its own margins and type size: 11-point text on A5 and 12-point text on A4.
  • The cover comes first, on a page of its own, then the chapters in the book’s reading order, each starting on a new page.
  • Keeps headings, paragraphs, bold, italic and underlined text, lists, block quotes, simple tables (with header rows repeated across pages) and pictures.
  • Follows the book’s own style sheets for alignment, bold and italic, font size, indents, margins and page breaks.
  • The table of contents becomes the PDF’s bookmarks, and the book’s title, author and language go into the PDF.
  • Fixed-layout books, such as comics and picture books, keep their pages: a single picture fills its page, and anything else is shrunk onto it.
  • Text stays searchable. It is set in Usugami’s own fonts, and your browser draws the characters they don’t have, such as Chinese or Arabic.

Your files stay on your device

Usugami works on your files right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: the files you pick and the PDFs it makes never leave your device.

There are no ads, no tracking and no cookies, and you don’t need an account. Tools you have used once keep working offline while your browser keeps their files. A few parts download the first time they’re needed, such as a text-recognition language, a font or the HEIC photo decoder, and need the internet then.

Questions

Can I convert e-books with DRM?

No. Usugami can’t read books protected with DRM (copy protection), so only books without DRM can be converted.

Should I choose A5 or A4?

A5, the default, is about the size of a paperback. A4 suits printing.

Does the PDF keep the table of contents?

Yes, as the PDF’s bookmarks, each leading to its place in the book. A book without a table of contents gets a bookmark for each chapter’s first main heading.

Which EPUB versions work?

Both EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 books can be converted.

Is Usugami free?

Yes. Usugami is free and open source, with no ads, no account and nothing to buy.

Does Usugami put its name on my PDFs?

Not on its pages, unless you turn on the Usugami watermark switch on the result screen, which adds a small “Usugami” in the corner of each page. Once on, it stays on for the PDFs you make next until you turn it off. The PDFs it makes do name Usugami in their document properties, as the program that made them.

Which browsers does it work in?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on phones, tablets and computers. Some buttons, such as Share and Save as…, show only in browsers that support them. On an Android phone you can also install the Usugami app.
