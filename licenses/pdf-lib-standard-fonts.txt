@pdf-lib/standard-fonts: the metrics and encodings of the 14 standard PDF fonts, part of
site/app/vendor/pdf-lib/pdf-lib.esm.min.js. The metrics are made from Adobe's Core 14 AFM files.
Source: https://github.com/Hopding/standard-fonts

pdf-lib does not ship the AFM files themselves: it ships metrics converted from them
(compressed JSON), which is a modification of those files.
